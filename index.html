How it works

1

Complete an offer

Surveys, app trials, and sign-ups from our offerwall partners — no purchase needed.

2

Earn data credits

Credits land in your wallet automatically, tracked in megabytes.

3

Redeem for eSIM data

Swap credits for a real eSIM data plan, scanned straight into your phone.
